Deep Cuts: your music taste, decoded.

Drop in your music library exports (Spotify, Apple Music, rekordbox and more) and get an explorable map of what you love: your eras and moods, the DJ lanes hiding in your library, and the sound you'd make as a producer. Every chart opens the songs behind it.

Your files never leave your browser. Everything is analyzed on your device. Nothing is uploaded, stored or shared, and closing the tab clears it.

About Deep Cuts

I'm Alina. I started DJing this fall and wanted to understand my own taste: what I actually save, how it's changed, and which songs would work in a set. Year-end recaps tell you what you played, but not why it all fits together. So I built the tool I wanted, then opened it up so anyone can run their own library through it.

Private by designYour files never leave your browser. There's no account, no server and no tracking. Close the tab and it's gone.
How it reads your musicGenres come from your music service, and tempo, key and mood come from Spotify's audio data. Every insight comes from fixed rules applied to your numbers, not AI guesses, and "How it works" in your report shows the math.
Built withClaude as a coding partner, plus a handful of open tools: Exportify, PapaParse, JSZip.

Ways to export your files

Spotify playlists via Exportify Recommended · all 3 reports

What it is. Exportify is a free, open-source website (code on GitHub) that downloads any Spotify playlist as a spreadsheet (CSV). Each row is a song with its genres, tempo (BPM), key, energy and mood scores, which is exactly what Deep Cuts needs.

How it works. You log in with Spotify's own login page and approve read-only access to your playlists. Exportify can't change anything in your account, and Deep Cuts never sees your Spotify login: you only hand Deep Cuts the files you download.

Unlocks. Listening, DJ and Producing. Time: about 2 minutes.

  1. Open exportify.net and click to log in with Spotify. Approve the permissions.
  2. Find Liked Songs at the top of your playlist list and click Export. This is your main library.
  3. Optional extras: your Your Top Songs 20XX playlists (Spotify Wrapped), a playlist of tracks you'd want to make (for Producing) and a playlist of tracks for a DJ set.
  4. The files land in your Downloads folder. Drop them all in the box below at once.
Spotify account data (listening history) Listening · play counts

What it is. Spotify's official copy of your data, requested from your account privacy page. It includes every song you've played and how long, which Exportify doesn't have. It has no genres, tempo or mood, so pair it with Exportify for the full picture.

Unlocks. "What you actually play": play counts, hours listened, songs you play but never saved. Time: 1–5 days for Spotify to email it (officially up to 30 for the full history).

  1. Go to spotify.com/account/privacy and scroll to Download your data.
  2. Tick Extended streaming history for your full lifetime history. "Account data" only covers the past year, but includes your library and playlists.
  3. Confirm the email Spotify sends, then wait for the second email with your download link.
  4. Drop the .zip file as is. Deep Cuts opens it and reads only the music files inside.
Apple Music library Listening · play counts

What it is. The Music app on a Mac can export your whole library as a file with every song's genre, play count, year and date added (Apple's guide). Your playlists come along too.

Unlocks. Listening (genres, eras, artists, play counts). No tempo or mood data, so DJ and Producing need another source. Time: 1 minute. Mac only.

  1. Open the Music app on your Mac.
  2. Choose File → Library → Export Library… and save the .xml file.
  3. Drop the .xml file below.
rekordbox collection (for DJs) DJ · accurate BPM & key

What it is. If you DJ with rekordbox, your collection file holds the tempo and key rekordbox analyzed from the audio, which are more accurate than Spotify's. Your playlists come along too, so a set playlist can feed the planner.

Unlocks. DJ lanes, Camelot wheel and set planner, plus genres and play counts for Listening. No energy or mood scores. Time: 1 minute.

  1. Open rekordbox on your computer.
  2. Choose File → Export Collection in xml format and save the file.
  3. Drop the .xml file below.
SoundCloud likes and playlists Listening basics

What it is. SoundCloud doesn't offer its own export, so use TuneMyMusic's SoundCloud-to-file tool. It logs into SoundCloud with your permission and saves your likes and playlists as a CSV file.

Unlocks. Your library and artists. The file has titles and artists only, so genres, tempo and mood will be missing. Pair it with Exportify or rekordbox for the full report. Time: about 5 minutes. Very large libraries may need a paid TuneMyMusic plan, so check their plans page.

  1. Open TuneMyMusic: SoundCloud to file.
  2. Connect your SoundCloud account and choose your liked tracks and any playlists.
  3. Pick Export to file (CSV) as the destination and download it.
  4. Drop the CSV below. Set a playlist to "DJ set playlist" in the list if it's your set.
Other services via TuneMyMusic or Soundiiz Listening basics

What it is. TuneMyMusic and Soundiiz move playlists between services and can save them as CSV files. Use them for YouTube Music, TIDAL, Amazon Music or Deezer.

Unlocks. Your library and artists. These files carry only titles and artists, so genres, tempo and mood will be missing. Best combined with another source. Time: 5 minutes.

  1. On TuneMyMusic, choose your service as the source, pick the playlists, then choose Export to file as the destination (example for YouTube Music).
  2. On Soundiiz, open a playlist and use its Export option to save a CSV.
  3. Drop the CSV files below.
Last.fm scrobbles Listening · history

What it is. If you use Last.fm, it has logged ("scrobbled") every song you've played across apps, often for many years. A free tool downloads that history.

Unlocks. Play counts and your listening timeline. Time: 1–2 minutes.

  1. Open the Last.fm data export tool.
  2. Enter your Last.fm username, choose Scrobbles and CSV, then click Go.
  3. Drop the CSV file below.
